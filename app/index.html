      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy on this list can be missed, according to PowerPyx as reported by GTA Zone and Keengamer. Everything else can be earned after the story, but the guides still report no mission replay, so keep manual saves.

      Why only one?

      The other 54 trophies are story progress, open-world activities, collectibles or money, all of which stay available after the credits. The single exception is a meditation spot offered in Chapter 1 that becomes unavailable when the chapter ends. Meditate there the first time it is offered and the risk is gone.
